## Accounts, access and database rules

- Acceptance (F2): the counsellor, a managing Domain Admin, a General or a System Admin decides, never the person (`private.can_accept`), only through `public.decide_learning(user, learning, 'accepted'|'changes', comment)` (security definer): accept, or request changes with a 10–500 character comment, which puts the learning back to in-progress (pct kept) for the learner to resubmit. It writes `learning_decisions` (who, name as it was, when, verdict, comment, evidence snapshot); learners have no write grant there. A learning completing itself is recorded as `self_completed` by a trigger (with the classroom feedback as its evidence). The evidence rule per format is `private.evidence_rule` (mirrored by `evidenceRule` in `src/logic/record.ts`; change both): Certification needs a link or certificate number; Book, Website, Mentoring, Coaching, Game, Micro-guide and Case write-up a reflection of at least 140 characters; the rest nothing. Triggers check it whenever the learner submits or edits a submission's evidence, stamp `submitted_at`/`updated_at`, freeze an accepted learning's evidence, and keep accepted items accepted. `public.waiting_submissions()` is the queue: counsellees' submissions plus, for anyone who may accept, those of people without a counsellor. Keep one permissive policy per table and action (merge with OR).
- Completion per learning type (owner, 2 Nov 2026; v0.7.2): Podcast (every chapter listened to its end; 80% before Task 3.5), Reel (a right answer), E-learning ("Mark as completed"), Avatar (three right answers in the evaluation quiz after the practice session; trust-based like reels) and Classroom complete themselves, without a counsellor: `private.is_self_complete()` lists these formats (published only). A Classroom completes when the learner sends the feedback form (rating 1–5 in `learner_learnings.evidence_rating`, "What will you apply?" of at least 40 characters in `evidence_note`; the record trigger refuses the learner's completion without it, `private.completion_rule()`/`completion_ok()`), or when the faculty registers attendance: `public.register_attendance(learning, user_ids)` (security definer; published Classroom only; all or nothing; faculty = `private.can_register_attendance()`: a Domain Admin for people they manage, a General or System Admin, never the person themselves) sets `accepted` with pct 100 and records verdict `attended`; an accepted item stays accepted. `public.attendance_roster(learning)` lists whom the caller may register. Every other format goes to acceptance with the evidence rule above. The app never offers "Ask … to approve" for a self-completing format (the database still accepts such a submission, so older ones keep waiting). The rules are `COMPLETION`, `FEEDBACK_MIN`, `REFLECTION_FORMATS`, `CERTIFICATE_FORMATS` in `src/logic/record.ts`; `src/logic/record.sync.test.ts` compares them with the latest migration, so change both together.
- Certification trajectories (design v4, part B; migration `20261117170000_trajectories.sql`). Three tables:
  - `trajectories` (ids `T1`–`T3` seeded from the design, new ones `T100`… from `trajectories_seq`): name, issuer, credential, enrol (`auto`/`open`/`approval`), months, valid_months (null: no expiry), renewal, cost, status (`draft`/`live`/`retired`), business_line (null: firm-wide; set in the editor's "Business line" select, `tj.line`/`tj.setLine`/`tj.lineOptions` in `trajectoryAdminVals.ts`, offering a Domain Admin only their lines), steps, maps, published_at (stamped the first time it goes live), updated_at. `steps` is an array of `{id, k: learn|elect|evidence|check|exam, lid?, opts?, pick?, title?, hours?, win, req, pre: stepId|null}`; the database checks only unique string ids and the kind (`private.trajectory_steps_ok`), the rest is checked in TS. `maps` is `[[audience, stage, mandatory|advised], …]` with the `curriculum_mappings` audience rule, a `career_stage` value, and `mandatory` only on a business-line, `ind:` or `tec:` audience (`amb:`/`pro:` only advise) (`private.trajectory_maps_ok`, replaced in `20261117190000_audience_members.sql`; TS `canBeMandatory`). Read: live ones by everyone signed in, drafts and retired ones by the line's content editors and by whoever can read an enrolment row on it. Write: `private.can_write_trajectory(line, status, maps)` = `can_edit_content(line)` and `can_edit_audience` for every mapped audience (as curriculum mappings), and a live trajectory with a mandatory map only by a System or General Admin (owner decision D2), so a Domain Admin cannot publish, edit, take back or delete one.
  - `trajectory_enrolments` (pk user, trajectory): status `requested`/`enrolled`, `start_month` (year*12 + month index), `steps` jsonb keyed by step id (`{state: submitted|booked|done, link?, ref?, note?}`, evidence, check and exam steps only; learning and elective steps are done when their learning is accepted), asked_at, decided_by, decided_at, created_at (added in `20261117200000_trajectory_decisions.sql`: leaving and starting again makes a new row, so older decisions are not shown on it). Read through `private.can_view_track` (the person, counsellor, managing admins, General Admins); no write grant: everything goes through the functions below. An `auto` trajectory binds only people inside one of its mandatory maps (business line or an `audience_members` membership, at the map's career stage, `private.in_mandatory_audience`): their row is written at their first action, starts in the publication month and cannot be withdrawn (D6, see `docs/decisions.md`); for everyone else it behaves like `open`. A retired trajectory still lets enrolled people finish and earn the credential (the learner functions and `decide_trajectory` do not check status).
  - `credentials`: name, issuer, earned_on, expires_on, link or certificate number (one is required; links only), trajectory_id, status `waiting`/`confirmed`, confirmed_by/at. Read through `can_view_track`; the learner inserts only their own `waiting` row (column grant without status, trajectory or confirmation) and may delete their own row while it is `waiting` (a confirmed one stays). Renewal (`20261117210000_credential_renewal.sql`): `renewal_of` (the credential a waiting renewal replaces; FK, on delete set null, indexed) and `superseded_at` (stamped when a confirmed renewal replaced the row); a partial unique index allows one waiting renewal per credential (a second is refused, 23505). Neither column is in the learner's insert grant. A superseded credential is no longer held: the wallet hides it, and "earned" (`credentialHeld`, `src/logic/trajectoryLearner.ts`) and the exam guard ignore it.
  - Functions (security definer): `request_trajectory(id)` (live only; `enrolled` for open and auto, `requested` for approval; asking again changes nothing), `withdraw_trajectory(id)` (deletes a requested row, or an enrolled one without a done step; never a mandatory auto-enrol one), `submit_trajectory_step(id, step, state 'submitted'|'booked', link, ref, note)` (evidence/check/exam only, booked for an exam only, an exam result needs a link or number and evidence a link or note, never on a submitted or done step, so the counsellor decides on what they saw; writes the enrolled row for a mandatory auto-enrol trajectory), `decide_trajectory(user, id, step|null, 'approve'|'talk', comment)` (by `private.can_accept`, as `decide_learning`; `talk` needs a comment of 10 to 500 characters, trimmed, `approve` takes an optional one with the same bounds; every decision is logged in `trajectory_decisions` (`20261117200000_trajectory_decisions.sql`, read through `can_view_track`, no client writes): a null step approves a start request, and `talk` leaves it waiting, stamped; a submitted step becomes done, and `talk` removes it so the learner resubmits; accepting the exam inserts the confirmed credential with expiry from valid_months, D5, and is refused while the person holds a valid confirmed credential from the same trajectory; the guard allows another credential after expiry, or once the old one is superseded; a signed-off exam step is not reopened, renewal is by proof, see `submit_renewal`), `submit_renewal(credential, link, ref, expires)` (the owner, a `confirmed`, not superseded credential with an expiry from 30 days before it and after it, `RENEW_DAYS`, sync-tested; a link or number with the proof rule; a trajectory credential takes no expiry, an external one needs a new expiry after today; inserts a `waiting` row copying name, issuer and trajectory, with `renewal_of`; the existing owner delete of a waiting row removes it), `confirm_credential(id)` (by `can_accept`; for a renewal, in the same transaction: earned today, a trajectory credential runs `valid_months` from today, and the renewed row is stamped `superseded_at`, refused when it already is). The exam guard ignores superseded rows (edited in place in `20261117200000_trajectory_decisions.sql`).
  - Learner side: library cards, the trajectory page (screen `trajectory`, AppState `trjView`/`trjOrigin`), the credentials wallet in My profile and the practice exam's "Open the trajectory" link. View-model `src/trajectoryVals.ts` (cards, exam link), `trajectoryPage.ts`, `walletVals.ts`, shared `trajectoryCtx.ts`; screens `Trajectory.tsx`, `Wallet.tsx`; pure rules `src/logic/trajectoryLearner.ts` (effective enrolment, `canWithdraw` mirrors `withdraw_trajectory`, `stepAction`, `credentialLook`).
