## Trajectory start month and who an auto-enrol trajectory binds (design v4, Task 5, 2026-11-17)

- **What.** An `auto` trajectory is mandatory only for a person inside one of its `mandatory` maps: the map's audience is the person's business line or one of their industry and technology memberships, and the map's stage is their career stage (`private.in_mandatory_audience`). For them the row is written at their first action and cannot be withdrawn; its `start_month` is the month the trajectory was published (`published_at`, or the current month if it has none). For everyone else an `auto` trajectory behaves like `open`. Rows for `open` and `approval` trajectories start in the current month, and approving a start request resets it to the approval month. Months are year * 12 + month index (UTC).
- **Why.** Owner decision D6 says the start is the later of the publication and the month the person joined that stage; the schema records no stage-since date, so the publication month is the closest rule the database can enforce, and it keeps the start stable whatever the person's first action is. `src/logic` (Task 6) mirrors this rule.
- **Alternatives.** Add `profiles.stage_since` (stamped when `career_stage` changes) and take the later of the two months; a production version should do this. ind:/tec: membership is set per person by the counsellor; see the next entry.

## Industry and technology membership makes ind:/tec: trajectory maps binding (design v4 follow-ups, Task 2, 2026-11-17)

- **What.** A person's industry (`ind:`) and technology (`tec:`) audiences are stored in `public.audience_members` (migration `20261117190000_audience_members.sql`). Their counsellor, their Domain Admins, General and System Admins set and remove them (`private.can_accept`); nobody sets their own, admins included. A trajectory map may be `mandatory` on a business-line, `ind:` or `tec:` audience (`private.trajectory_maps_ok`); `amb:` and `pro:` stay advised only. A mandatory map binds a person at the map's stage when its audience is their business line or one of their memberships (`private.in_mandatory_audience`, mirrored by `inMandatoryAudience` in `src/logic/trajectory.ts`). Removing a membership frees the person: they may leave the trajectory while no step is signed off.
- **Why.** The design marks industries and technologies "Mandatory or advised"; the counsellor knows what a person works on, and the existing accept rule (`can_accept`) already names who decides about a person.
- **Alternatives.** Derive membership from staffing data (charged hours per sector, skill tags), as the design's lens descriptions say; let people declare their own (refused: a person could then leave a mandatory trajectory by removing themselves).
- **PoC shortcuts.** No change log beyond `set_by` and `set_at`; the audience catalogue is `AUDS` in TypeScript (the database checks the format only, as for `curriculum_mappings`); membership does not yet steer single learnings (see the shortcut table). Production: a staffing data feed with versioned membership changes, and the catalogue in a table.

## Flash cards and practice exams are drafted in the upload form, not a studio (design v4, Task 12, 2026-11-17)

- **What.** Flash cards and Practice exam are contribution formats (SQL lists and TS lists change together; `src/logic/items.test.ts` checks the migration). A contribution keeps its items in `contributions.items` (jsonb) and publishes them as `learnings.content`. Items come from AI (`draft_items`, off by default), a scripted fallback for cards only (top passages of the picked bundles, labelled "Scripted from the bundle", never AI) or a CSV. A published deck sets `due` to its size and `next` to empty; a published exam sets `bank` to its size and a fixed `real` text.
- **Why.** Owner decision D1 (default): only the flash card and exam studio, text only; this is its upload-form half. Exam questions cannot be scripted from a passage without inventing wrong options, so they need AI or a CSV.
- **Shortcuts and production replacements.** No spaced-repetition schedule (`due`/`next` are placeholders), no `real` exam description from the author, no per-item review comments (reviewers judge the whole set), CSV rows cannot span lines. A production version would add per-item review, an author-written exam description and multi-line CSV fields.

## Owner answers of 17 Nov 2026 on the design v4 gaps (follow-ups to design v4)

Recorded from the owner's answers; implemented on branch `feature/design-v4` (version 0.9.0).

- **F1 Business line on trajectories.** *What:* the trajectory editor has a "Business line" select: Firm-wide, Consulting, Audit or Tax & Legal for a System or General Admin; a Domain Admin sees only the lines they administer (no Firm-wide). *Why:* the column and `private.can_write_trajectory` already existed; only the editor lacked the field. *Alternative:* derive the line from the maps (refused: a trajectory can be mapped across audiences while owned by one line).
- **F2 Industry and technology membership is set by the counsellor.** *What:* `public.audience_members` holds a person's `ind:` and `tec:` audiences; their counsellor, their Domain Admins, General and System Admins set and remove them, nobody their own. Membership makes a `mandatory` trajectory map on that audience bind the person (details in "Industry and technology membership makes ind:/tec: trajectory maps binding" above, which replaces the earlier rule that `mandatory` was only for business-line audiences). *Why:* it matches the owner's matrix of roles against audiences (Q4) for the one place that needs it now. *Alternative:* let people declare their own (refused: they could leave a mandatory trajectory by removing themselves), or a staffing data feed.
- **F3 "Talk first" needs a comment the learner sees.** *What:* on a trajectory start request or step, "Talk first" takes a comment of 10 to 500 characters (`decide_trajectory`, 22023 otherwise); "Approve" takes an optional one with the same bounds. Every decision is logged in `public.trajectory_decisions` (read through `private.can_view_track`, written only by the function), and the learner sees the latest talk comment on the step or the start request. `trajectory_enrolments.created_at` lets a re-created enrolment ignore decisions from an earlier one. *Why:* the learner needs to know what to talk about, as with `decide_learning`. *Alternative:* a free "reason" with no minimum (refused: it would stay empty).
- **F4 Credential renewal by proof.** *What:* from 30 days before a confirmed credential's expiry (and after it) the learner submits proof, a link or a certificate number (`public.submit_renewal`); the counsellor confirms (`confirm_credential`); a new credential with a new expiry replaces the old one in the wallet (`credentials.renewal_of`, `superseded_at`). One waiting renewal per credential. A trajectory credential runs `valid_months` from the day it is confirmed; an external one takes the new expiry date the learner gave. *Why:* it reuses the evidence rule and the counsellor as the one gate (D5). *Alternative:* reopen the exam step (refused: the history of the first credential would be lost).

**Deploy note.** Push all v4 and follow-up migrations in one `supabase db push`. `20261117200000_trajectory_decisions.sql` (the exam guard) references `credentials.superseded_at`, a column added by `20261117210000_credential_renewal.sql`; plpgsql resolves it at run time, so the order inside one push is fine, but pushing the first alone leaves the exam guard failing on every exam sign-off.

## Owner decisions D1–D6 (design v4): defaults adopted 17 Nov 2026, awaiting owner confirmation

The owner did not answer these before the build, so the plan's defaults apply (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-17-design-v4.md`, Task 0). Each is also in `docs/product/user-journeys-and-epics.md` §7.

- **D1 Studios.** *What:* only the Flash card & Exam studio (text only, through `complete()`), plus the upload form; the other nine studios are parked. *Why:* the others need voice, video or avatar generation the app does not have; the avatar studio is parked for Taxatar. *Alternative:* build more studios once a generation service is chosen.
- **D2 Publishing a mandatory trajectory.** *What:* a System or General Admin publishes it; a Domain Admin publishes advised trajectories for their own line only (`private.can_write_trajectory`). *Why:* the design's "Learning Council sign-off" has no counterpart in the app. *Alternative:* a council role with its own approval step.
- **D3 Completion of the new formats.** *What:* Flash cards, Job aid and Practice exam complete themselves (deck reviewed once, all items ticked, one finished round); Teach-back once an explanation is sent; Webinar like Classroom; Case exercise and Stretch assignment go to the counsellor (a link, or a 140-character reflection). *Why:* the same split as the owner's rules of 2 Nov 2026: self-study completes itself, work products are accepted. *Alternative:* manual "Mark as completed" for the four self-completing formats.
- **D4 Practice-exam readiness bar.** *What:* 70 %, as advice only; it never locks the exam step. *Why:* the design's value; locking would add a rule the database does not hold. *Alternative:* a per-trajectory bar that locks booking.
- **D5 How a trajectory ends in a credential.** *What:* the learner submits the exam result with a certificate link or number, the counsellor accepts, and the credential is added with its expiry from "valid for"; external credentials wait for the counsellor, and the owner may remove one while it waits. Links only, no uploads. *Why:* reuses the Certification evidence rule and the counsellor as the one gate. *Alternative:* confirmation by the issuer (an integration) or uploads to Storage.
- **D6 Auto-enrol start date.** *What:* a person in an auto-enrol mandatory audience is enrolled without a row; the start month is the later of the publication and the month they joined that stage, and a row is written at their first action. *Why:* no rows to back-fill when a trajectory goes live. *Alternative:* write rows at publication. The database uses the publication month until a stage-since date exists (see "Trajectory start month" above).

## Studios Phase 2, Task 2.2: Explainer Studio and the Interactive explainer format

- **The check's questions live in the items, after the steps.** *What:* an explainer's `items` are its step rows (`[label, text]`) followed by none or exactly three exam questions (`{t, q, o, a, why}`), checked by the one question rule `private.question_ok` that Practice exam now uses too; the cap of 10 covers both, the minimum of 3 counts steps only. *Why:* the questions need four options, an answer and a reason, which a three-text row cannot hold; the exam question shape and its checks already exist. *Alternative:* a new column for the check (refused: another field for the fingerprint, guard and grants), or encoding options in a row's text (refused: fragile).
- **The check comes only from Draft with AI.** *What:* the AI is asked for three questions only when "End with?" is the check; they cannot be typed in, only removed as a whole. Without an AI connection the check ending is not offered (the chip group says why). *Why:* the brief: check questions come from the draft or are omitted. *Alternative:* a question editor (not needed for a proof of concept; the Flash studio's exam questions are not hand-typed either).
- **The ending and the check agree.** Ending with the check needs its questions, and questions need that ending; the Draft step and `contribution_problems` say so in the same words. The published content has the check only on that ending.
- **Explainer options kept and removed (honest UI).** Kept: "Depth?" (captioned "Steers the AI draft"; it also shows in the panel's subtitle) and "End with?" (published). Removed: "Style?" (the panel is a step-through only; "Clickable diagram" and "Scroll story" are not drawn), the "Look" row (the panel has one look; "Firm colours"/"Line drawing" would need two), the "Narration" row ("Voice-over" comes in Phase 3, leaving one option). With no Produce rows or cast, the Explainer has five steps.
- **Completion 'mark', done by the panel.** The last step seen completes an explainer without a check; with a check, finishing the check does (best score kept in `detail.best`). The last step counts as seen when the learner arrives at it or moves on from it, so a one-step explainer completes on "Done"; the ending (check or summary) opens only after that. `COMPLETION` and `private.is_self_complete` list it.
- **The check ending stays honest when AI is off.** A draft that already chose the check keeps it on screen with the reason; the Draft step says "only Draft with AI writes them and it is not switched on" then, and "Draft with AI again (it rewrites the steps as well)" when it is on. The database names the problem only ("needs its three questions").
- **One question round.** The practice exam, the explainer's check and the Flash studio's play-test run the same view-model (`fmtRound.ts`) and component (`QuestionRound.tsx`); the play-test's own copy is gone.
- **SVG sanitiser copied from Horizon and hardened.** `src/logic/sanitizeSvg.ts` (with Horizon's tests) differs from Horizon's after two security reviews: the attribute names id, href, begin and end are written and compared in lower case; every id is prefixed; a reference (#id href, url(#id), SMIL begin/end) to an id the drawing does not define is dropped; SMIL timing is an allow-list (offsets, clock values, `indefinite`, events of the drawing's own ids); any url() other than a plain url(#id) drops its attribute or style declaration. Fix round: title and desc keep text only (they are HTML integration points); attributes are judged by local name and kept only without a namespace (but xlink:href); an animation target is checked however it is spelt and an animation without a plain target is dropped; `src()` and the CSS tokens that run or import drop the whole declaration. A drawing reaches the page only through `SafeSvg.tsx`, which sanitises it and gives each rendered copy its own id prefix (React `useId`). The exam content reader uses the one question rule `isQuestion` (four options), as `private.question_ok` does. The explainer studio makes no drawings yet (`svg` is optional in the content); Motion (Task 2.3) reuses the sanitiser. The panel draws an SVG in a fixed 640 x 320 view box, as Horizon does.

## Studios Phase 2, Task 2.3: Motion Studio and the Animated flow format

- **The drawing lives in the items, after the stages.** *What:* an Animated flow's `items` are its stage rows (`[label, text]`, at most 7) followed by one drawing `{svg, alt, made, seconds}` (`private.motion_ok`, `private.drawing_ok`; `isDrawing` in `src/logic/motionSvg.ts`). The cap of 8 covers both. *Why:* the explainer's pattern: the check fingerprint, the client-name scan (text alternative and drawing text), the guard's lock and the save path cover it with no new column. *Alternative:* a `contributions.media` column (refused: a new grant, fingerprint signature, guard and five copied functions).
- **Three real build steps, one AI call.** "Drawing the flow" lays the stages out as scripted boxes and arrows (`made: 'layout'`, never sendable); "Animating each stage" is the one AI call (`studio_draft` through `Logic.viaAi`, Horizon's `RENDER_SYSTEM` adapted as `MOTION_SYSTEM`, the layout and the stage cues as data), or without AI the scripted build-in of the same boxes; "Writing the text alternative" writes it from the stages. A failed animating step keeps the layout and is retried alone; "Continue building" then runs the steps still to do. *Why:* the brief's "steps 1 and 2 are one AI call" while every step does real work and a retry has something to keep. *Alternative:* steps 1 and 2 both inside the AI call (refused: step 2 would have nothing of its own to retry).
- **Without AI: a scripted, animated flow (not a static one).** The brief preferred a static scripted SVG labelled "Scripted · static". The scripted flow builds its boxes in and rings each stage at its cue instead, labelled "Scripted · boxes and arrows". *Why:* the player, its stage caption and "played to the end" mean something only on a timeline; a still picture under a 45-second timer would not. It is still scripted (never "Made with AI") and the Produce step says why it is.
- **The AI drawing is read strictly.** The whole answer must be one `<svg` of at most 60,000 characters that survives the sanitiser, with 1 to 30 animations (`readMotionSvg`); it is stored as returned and sanitised whenever it is drawn (`SafeSvg`). ## The Avatar's guardrails (Studios Phase 6, Task 6.3)

- **Two deterministic layers always on; the classifiers fail open** (Phase 6 ruling, owner question 9; Taxatar's choice). The patterns (input and output) never depend on a provider; a classifier that times out (input 4,000 ms, output 1,500 ms), errors or returns malformed JSON lets the line or reply through and is logged as `classifier_unavailable` with its layer, never its content. *Alternative:* fail closed (a provider outage would stop every practice run). *Shortcut (PoC):* a production version records the classifier outcome on the turn (so a counsellor can see that a line was reviewed by patterns only) and alerts on a rising fail-open rate. The UI never claims an AI review: it shows only held lines.
- **The patterns read the raw line.** Taxatar's chat path sanitised first, which redacted "ignore previous instructions" before the pattern for it ran, so only the classifier caught it. Here the patterns read the trimmed raw line and the model is shown the sanitised one.
- **Metering: one `ai_count_call` per turn under `avatar_conversation`**, its characters covering the persona ask, the input classifier ask and the output classifier ask at its 1,200-character cap (the brief's rule), so the daily 120 calls and 1,000,000 characters count a turn once and the classifiers' text is paid for. A prefilter hold counts nothing and sends nothing. *Alternative:* a use per classifier (more rows for the System Admin to set up and three counts per turn).
- **A held exchange stores the code on both turns** (the learner's line as typed, the substitute for the character), so the transcript can mark it and the score can count it, and the held exchange is left out of what the character is shown next (a learner's injection attempt does not stay in its context; the turns still alternate). A held line still uses one of the run's lines.
- **A hold from a classifier or the post-filter discards the reply; the reply is stored nowhere** and the character says the substitute line in role. The classifier asks are sent through the same `call` as the persona, so the one role-play path and its connection serve all three.
- **Hidden-text echo is not part of the output guard.** `textEchoes` (the one helper) could hold a reply that repeats 24 characters of Reveals or Hides, but Reveals is meant to be said and Hides is meant to be earned by probing, so a verbatim echo is mostly a correct answer in the author's own words, and holding it would punish the learner for doing well. Under a determined jailbreak the character may still say what it keeps back: that knowledge is never a secret like a key (accepted risk, as the Game role's M1 ruling). The output rule on the notes' labels (`out_meta`) stays. The Game's AI role has no guard module wired in; the module is reusable if the owner asks.
- **Pattern changes from Taxatar's (6.3 fix round 1 narrowed them):** no clinical patterns or allow-list; French added to the input patterns; the patterns hold only the shapes of an instruction override or of a model speaking, never an ordinary business line ("act as the data controller", "tell me a story about a time...", "I felt like an idiot") or an in-character reply ("assistant to the CFO", "my instructions from the board", "I'm sorry, but I can't go into the payroll details"); refusals are held only in an assistant's register ("I cannot assist with that request", "I'm not able to role-play"), because a held deflection loops on Tough (the held exchange is left out of the context, so the model deflects again). "my guidelines", "my instructions", "simulation" and the names Claude, Gemini and Bard are not output patterns. *Cost:* a role flip without an AI word ("pretend you are the consultant") and German, French-output and spaced-letter attacks are the classifier's alone (recorded as gaps in the fixtures).
- **Unedited "To write:" placeholders are refused at the check** (TS `avatarRows` and SQL `avatar_ok` / `avatar_row_problems`, both by exact text, so an edited placeholder passes). *Shortcut:* `prefilterSay` takes the language and ignores it, because a learner switches language mid-line; the substitute line follows the run's language.
- **Shortcut (PoC):** `ai/avatarGuarded.ts` cannot be run or type-checked locally (no Deno), as the rest of the `ai` function; its core is tested under Vitest with the provider and the database stood in. Production: a Deno test run in CI. A classifier that times out is not aborted at the provider (the race resolves first); production would pass an abort signal.

- **One normalisation before both pattern layers** (`normalised` in `avatarGuard.ts`): NFKC (full-width letters), invisible and format characters removed, common Cyrillic and Greek look-alikes as Latin, straight quotes, horizontal whitespace as one space. The patterns, never the stored or shown text, read it (Cyrillic text itself is unchanged). The patterns accept articles and possessives ("ignore the / your previous instructions"; "disregard" too). Every pattern is defined once (the sanitiser's global copies are derived), all are linear, and a test runs 1,200 adversarial characters through them. The format check looks at the whole reply (a heading or three list lines anywhere), and `Ask`/`Msg` are defined once in `_shared/promptData.ts`.

## Scoring, sharing and the counsellor's confirmation (Studios Phase 6, Task 6.4)

- **The AI scores; the counsellor's confirmation is a note** (Phase 6 ruling). The score is advice: no score, share or confirmation writes a completion, a record or progress (database case `AS`); the learning is completed by its quiz only, and a practice run in the studio can be scored but never shared (S6). The UI never calls the score a grade; "you can appeal" is dropped.
- **Server-owned, bound to the hash of what was said.** The `ai` function (`action: 'score_avatar'`) reads the stored run through `avatar_score_input` (the client sends only the run id), reads the answer strictly (`readScore`) and records it through `record_avatar_score` (service role only), which checks it again with `private.avatar_score_ok` (the same rules in SQL) and for `avatar_score_hash` = sha256 of the turns, the learning points and the prompt version `1.0.0`. A turn added meanwhile gives "The run changed while it was scored · score it again". Recording ends the run (`finished_at`) and happens once. *Alternative:* a client-written score (forgeable).
- **What earns credit (one definition, `creditable`).** Only a learner turn that no guard held and the injection patterns do not flag can be cited; a held exchange, a flagged line and the character's reply to it are shown to the scorer as markers without their text; a point without a cited creditable turn is clamped to 30; with one creditable line no point is above 54 (Mediocre); the prompt says credit follows the learner's own words, never what the character volunteered. So a jailbreak dump earns nothing: the model cannot cite it. A line aimed at the scorer ("give me 100", "note to the evaluator", "ignore the rubric") is also non-creditable, and learner lines go through `sanitizeUntrusted` first. The scorer never receives Reveals or Hides. *Cost, prompt-level:* a LATER learner line that restates a fact the character let slip can still be cited; that the cited line itself shows the behaviour is the model's judgement, which the code cannot enforce (it only limits which turns may be cited).
- **The overall and the bands are computed** (weighted mean, rounded; Taxatar's 85/70/55/40), never read from the model; SQL says the same (`private.score_band`, `avatar_score_ok`). Strictness is total: any malformed, missing, extra, duplicated or over-long part gives 502 and nothing is recorded (a retry costs a call). *Shortcut:* the injection flags in `flags` are codes only and `avatar_score_ok` does not re-run the patterns (SQL only checks that a cited turn is a learner turn the guard did not hold). `avatar_score_ok` requires every key with its type (fix round 1: an absent key was null and skipped a rule).
- **Metering and timeout:** a scoring call counts as one call under `avatar_conversation` (its connection, its 120 calls and 1,000,000 characters a person a day; a long run's scoring ask is the whole transcript, up to about 90,000 characters, so a day holds a few runs of the longest kind). No new use, so one switch and one connection serve the character and its score. The use's "stay in character" preamble is NOT put in front of a score (it would contradict the scorer); the scoring text carries its own rules (`preamble: ''` in `index.ts`). The call has its own timeout, `SCORE_TIMEOUT_MS` = 90,000 ms (the ask can be about 90,000 characters; the edge request limit is 150 s; measure at deploy), not the 30 s default. *Alternative:* a use `avatar_score` (clean, but a second connection to set up before a run can be scored). *Shortcut (PoC):* production would add the use.
- **Who reads a shared run: the waiting_submissions rule (fix round 1).** `private.is_decider_for`: the learner's counsellor, or an admin who may accept when the learner has no counsellor (ONE rule: `waiting_submissions` is redefined in the same unpushed migration to call it, with the same result for evidence submissions); it governs the list (`shared_avatar_runs`: unconfirmed, oldest first, the first 50 with the true number waiting ("Showing the oldest 50 of N"; the badge counts N), without the transcript and the flags), the transcript read on open (`shared_avatar_run_turns`: a held line's text blanked, so the held words never reach the counsellor's browser) and the confirmation. The `avatar_runs` read policy is now the learner's own only. An admin therefore cannot pre-empt the counsellor, and no admin sees a transcript the learner did not mean for them.
- **Sharing is the learner's choice; withdrawing is allowed until the counsellor confirms** (owner ruling: privacy first): `withdraw_avatar_run`; after the confirmation it stays, because the counsellor's note is on it. The learner's share and withdraw buttons and score screen come with the panel (6.6); `LEARNER_SCORE_NOTE` and `AvatarScoreCard` are made for it.
- **Confirmation** (`confirm_avatar_run`): by the decider, once, a note of at most `NOTE_MAX` (600) characters (optional); it appears on "Waiting for you" as a second kind of row (one `QueueRow` shell serves both kinds) and the run leaves the queue when confirmed. The badge counts the runs waiting.
- **One lookup, one comment box.** `private.avatar_run_contribution` (added to the unpushed 20261118200000) is the one place a run finds its scenario: the character's context and the score both use it. `queueHelpers.ts` holds what both kinds of row share (first name, initials, the decisions in flight, the comment box with its own maximum).
- **Shortcut (PoC):** `ai/avatarScore.ts` cannot be run or type-checked locally (no Deno); its flow is tested under Vitest with the provider and the database stood in. Production: a Deno test run in CI.

- **Shortcuts (PoC), with the production version:** (1) the LiveAvatar calls match the public reference (the token answer carries `data.session_id`; `POST /v1/sessions/stop {session_id}`, a 2xx whose body `code` is 100 or 1000 counts as stopped; anything else fails closed); if live behaviour differs every session is charged in full and blocks a second one until its window passes, so check it at the first live run; (2) the `avatar` function cannot be type-checked or run locally (no Deno), tested through `core.ts` under Vitest; (3) the look drawer edits only the HeyGen ids of an existing look ("Edit live video"); the name, portrait, colour and licence are not editable; (4) `useLiveAvatar` starts a session in an effect: in development React StrictMode mounts twice and the two opens race, so about half the time the dev video says "Another video session of yours is still open" (production mounts once); the video attaches on the SDK's stream-ready event, `speak` returns false while the avatar cannot take a line, and the final report uses an access token cached at connect.

## Apply: placeholder steps (Studios Phase 7, Task 7.4)

- **Apply writes through the editor's one path, after a confirmation.** *What:* `applyToTrajectory` calls `patch` (refusal by `refusal()` first, state now, `saveTrajectorySoon` later). The button reads "Apply N accepted modules" and opens a list of what will be added and skipped ("Apply adds steps; it never removes or changes one."); only the second press writes. *Idempotent:* every step made carries `from: 'p:<module id>'`; the ids are made once outside `change` (which may run twice); the change is computed from the trajectory as it is when it runs (the saved trajectory is the one the screen shows, so a title edit made right after is saved together with the steps). ## Task 7.5: Draft with AI from a placeholder
- **No creation rule for placeholder drafts (owner, 18 Nov 2026).** *What*: editors of a trajectory draft its placeholders' material in the matching studio for the trajectory's line without a creation rule; the contribution carries the placeholder (`for_trajectory`, `for_step`). *Why*: owner answer. *Alternatives*: a creation rule per editor (refused by the owner); a flag on the editor (not tied to one placeholder).
- **Tight grant and full review (controller ruling, security review).** The grant binds line, format, domain (the step's `dom`, else `prof`) and no audience, so a drafter cannot pick a domain or audience that routes to a weaker review requirement; a grant draft always gets the full default review (2 peers, expert, quality & risk); submit re-checks the right to plan (the caller is the author) and publish re-checks it for the author (`user_can_plan_trajectory`, impersonation as in `plan_trajectory_allowed`). *Alternative*: store the author's rights on the contribution (stale).
- **Linking needs the placeholder's own draft.** `link_made_steps` links only a contribution drafted for that trajectory and step; before, any published contribution a `cid` named linked. The 7.4 `TM` cases were updated.
